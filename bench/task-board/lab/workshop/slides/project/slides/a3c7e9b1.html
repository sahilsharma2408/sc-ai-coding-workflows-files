Why sub-agents. One long job keeps filling a single context window, and as it grows the model loses track of instructions and makes more mistakes. A frontier model copes. Small local models cannot, because their windows are smaller and a messy context hurts them more. The fix is to split the job. An orchestrator plans and hands small, precise jobs to sub-agents. Each sub-agent starts with a fresh context for its job and reports back. In the report, the turns column shows the main agent and the sub-agents separately, for example 7 plus 3.
